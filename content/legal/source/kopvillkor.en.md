## 3. How to buy

You can choose between two delivery methods at checkout:

- **Shared pallet.** Lower bottle price and shipping. Your order is shipped together with others when the pallet is full. No amount is charged until the pallet closes. See below.
- **Direct delivery.** The bottle price is 5% higher and shipping is 149 kr per box of 6 bottles. Your card is charged when you buy and the wine is normally delivered within 5–10 working days. See section 3 b.

### How a shared pallet works

PACT combines several orders into one shared shipment from the producers in France to Sweden. That is why the wine can cost what it does. It also means that things work a little differently than in an ordinary online shop.

**Step 1. You buy.** You choose wines and complete the purchase at checkout. **The purchase is binding when you click the buy button.** Your card is saved with our payment provider Stripe, but no amount is charged yet. You receive an order confirmation with these terms by email.

**Step 2. The pallet fills up.** The pallet is virtual at first. You can see how many bottles have been ordered and how many are needed for the pallet to be shipped.

**Step 3. The pallet closes and your card is charged.** The pallet closes when it has reached the threshold for shipping. **Only then is your card charged** with the amount shown at checkout. You receive a receipt by email.

**Step 4. Delivery.** Your order is packed in boxes at the producers in France, transported to Sweden and delivered to your home with an ID check (see section 7).

### Cancelling before the pallet closes

Until the pallet closes, you can **cancel your purchase free of charge**, via your account or by emailing customer@pactwines.com. No amount is charged in that case. After delivery, you also have a right of withdrawal under section 9.

### If the pallet takes time to fill

There is no time limit for when the pallet has to be full. **Your order stays in place until the pallet closes or until you cancel it yourself.** You do not need to do anything to keep it. No amount is charged until the pallet closes. In your account you can see how close the pallet is to the threshold, and we email you regular updates, with a link where you can cancel.

When an order is cancelled, your saved card is removed, unless you choose to keep it for a future purchase.

### 3 b. Direct delivery

**You buy and pay.** The purchase is binding when you click the buy button. Your card is charged immediately with the total price at checkout. You receive an order confirmation with these terms and a receipt by email.

**Delivery.** Your boxes are packed in France and we send them to your home with UPS. Delivery time is normally **5–10 working days** from when the order is confirmed and paid. If you have not received your delivery within 30 days, you may cancel the purchase. You will then get the full amount back within 14 days.

**Age check.** UPS checks at handover that the recipient is at least 20 years old. Show valid ID. If the age check fails, the wine will not be handed over.

**If the box cannot be handed over.** If UPS cannot hand over the box, for example because no one is home, the box is not collected or the age check fails, it is sent back to the producer. Once the box is back, we refund what you paid for the wine, but not the shipping. If you still want the wine, we will send it again for a new shipping fee of SEK 149 per box. If the box did not arrive because of us or UPS, you instead get the full amount back including shipping, or a new delivery at no cost. Your right of withdrawal applies as usual: if you cancel the purchase before the box has been delivered, you also get the shipping fee back, but we deduct the actual cost of returning the box.

**Right of withdrawal.** You have the same right of withdrawal as for a shared pallet, see section 9. You can withdraw from the purchase even before delivery.

### 3 c. Boxes of 6 bottles

The wine is packed in boxes of 6 bottles at each producer or wholesaler. That is why the number of bottles from the same producer or wholesaler must be 6, 12, 18 and so on. You may mix different wines from the same producer or wholesaler in one box, and you can buy from several in the same order. The cart shows if a box is not full.

### If a producer cannot deliver

Sometimes a wine sells out after you have bought.

- We will first offer you an **equivalent replacement wine** from the same producer or wholesaler. The wine is replaced only if you accept it.
- If the wine can be removed without leaving a box incomplete, you may choose to remove it instead. You will then get your money back for that wine, or you will not be charged for it if you have not been charged yet.
- If the wine cannot be removed without leaving a box incomplete and you do not accept the replacement wine, **the entire order is cancelled**. You will then get the full amount back without undue delay and no later than within 14 days, or you will not be charged at all if you have not been charged yet.

## 8. If delivery cannot be completed

For shared pallets: If you are not at home, cannot show ID or are not at least 20 years old, our delivery partner will make a new delivery attempt. If delivery cannot be completed then either, the goods are returned. For direct delivery, point 3 b applies.

In the event of a return, you pay the actual cost of return shipping, without any mark-up. If the handover is refused at the age check, we do not charge any fee.

You may examine the goods in the same way as you would in a shop, but no more than that. If a bottle has been opened, or stored or handled in a way that may have damaged the wine, for example in heat or direct sunlight, you are responsible for the reduction in value. We may then make a deduction from the refund corresponding to the reduction in value, up to the full price of the bottle. This follows from Chapter 2, § 14 of lagen (2005:59) om distansavtal och avtal utanför affärslokaler (the Swedish Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act).

## 9. Right of withdrawal

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal under lagen (2005:59) om distansavtal och avtal utanför affärslokaler. The withdrawal period starts the day after you have received the wine. If you buy several bottles that are delivered on different occasions, the period starts when you have received the last one. If you want to withdraw from the purchase, email customer@pactwines.com, or use the standard withdrawal form from Konsumentverket (the Swedish Consumer Agency), within the withdrawal period. Then send the goods back no later than 14 days after you notified us. We refund the full amount, including the original delivery cost for standard delivery, no later than 14 days after we received your notice. We may wait to make the refund until we have received the goods back. Return shipping and reduction in value are covered in section 8. Until the pallet closes, you can also cancel the purchase free of charge, see section 3.

## 10. Complaints

If the wine is faulty, for example corked, damaged on delivery or not what you ordered, you have the right to make a complaint under konsumentköplagen (2022:260) (the Swedish Consumer Sales Act). The right to complain applies for three years from delivery. Notify us within a reasonable time after you discover the fault. If you notify us within two months, this is always considered to be in time. Email customer@pactwines.com and feel free to attach a photo. If the fault is accepted, you get a new bottle, or your money back if that is not possible, and we pay for any return shipping. If we cannot agree, you can turn to Allmänna reklamationsnämnden, the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes (arn.se).
